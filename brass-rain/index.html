    How this game works

    Seven lines, then you're playing.

    1. Pull the handle. Balls arc over the top and rain down through the nails.
    2. Pockets pay. A ball that drops into a pocket pays balls back. Most balls miss, so playing straight slowly loses.
    3. The slot in the middle spins the reels. That narrow mouth is the one worth aiming at.
    4. Three matching reels start a BONUS. The jackpot pocket swings open and pays hard for the next hundred balls or so. This is how you get ahead.
    5. Between rounds you shop. Balls you haven't launched buy parts at the workbench, and you can bend nails to steer the rain toward the slot.
    6. Cash balls out for coins. Coins buy machines for your arcade, and those earn coins around the clock, even while you're away.
    7. When a row of doors lights up, click one. Right pays as many times over as there are doors, wrong pays nothing, and left alone the paying door opens by itself.
